I'm **ch0tix** — a computer-science engineer and security researcher.

I build software and I break it. My day-to-day lives somewhere between
writing clean, reliable systems and poking holes in other people's: reverse
engineering, exploit development, application and infrastructure security, and
CTF competitions when there's time.

### what I do

- **Offensive security** — web & binary exploitation, pentesting, red-teaming
- **Reverse engineering** — static & dynamic analysis, malware triage, tooling
- **Software engineering** — systems programming, automation, secure-by-design
- **Research & writeups** — I document what I learn here on the blog

### this site

It runs in two modes. The one you're reading now is the **graphic** blog.
There's also a full-screen **console** mode — select **`/console`** in the menu
(top-right) and type `help`. Everything on the site is reachable from the command
line; type `gui` or press `Esc` to come back.
