Welcome to my corner of the internet.

I'm a computer-science engineer and security researcher, and this is where I'll
publish writeups, research notes, tooling, and the occasional rant about
software that should know better.

Expect posts on:

- **web & binary exploitation** — the fun kind of debugging
- **reverse engineering** — taking things apart to understand them
- **CTF writeups** — challenges, solutions, and the rabbit holes along the way
- **secure engineering** — building things that don't fall over when poked

<!--more-->

### why two modes?

Because a security blog that *only* looks normal is a missed opportunity. Hit
the backtick key `` ` `` (or the `>_ console` button) and you drop into a real
command line. Type `help`, `posts`, `about`, `open 1`. It's the same content —
just a different way in.
